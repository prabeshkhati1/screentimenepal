Questions? Answers.

How do I place an order?

Pick a service and plan, add it to your bag, and send the ready-made order message on WhatsApp or Instagram. We confirm the plan and availability before you pay.

How can I pay?

eSewa, Khalti and bank transfer/QR can be arranged in chat. Ask for current, verified payment details in the chat. This website never collects payments.

What's the difference between Shared and Private?

For Gemini AI Pro, the Shared plan is the best value with full Pro features, while a Private profile gives you your own profile for a more personal experience. Ask in chat how setup works for your plan.

When will I get my subscription?

Delivery timing and access method depend on the plan. Confirm both in chat before making a payment. This website does not activate subscriptions automatically.

Are these official subscriptions?

ScreenTime Nepal is an independent reseller, not affiliated with the brands shown. Ask about the specific plan, account arrangement and applicable terms before ordering.

Can I change or renew my plan later?

Message us to discuss changes or renewal. Nothing renews automatically through this website.

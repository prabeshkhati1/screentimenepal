GOOD QUESTIONS / 04

Wondering
about something?

Here's the short version. For anything else, just send a message.

How do I place an order? +

Pick a service and duration, add it to your bag, and send the pre-filled order message on WhatsApp. We'll confirm the plan and availability before you pay.

How can I pay? +

eSewa, Khalti and bank transfer/QR can be arranged in chat. Ask for current, verified payment details from the seller; do not send money to details supplied by this website.

When will I get my subscription? +

Delivery timing and access method depend on the plan. Confirm both in WhatsApp before making a payment. This website does not activate subscriptions automatically.

Are these official subscriptions? +

ScreenTime Nepal is an independent reseller concept, not affiliated with the brands shown. Ask the seller about the specific plan, account arrangement and applicable terms before ordering.

Can I change my plan later? +

Contact the seller to discuss changes or renewal. Nothing is automatically renewed by this website.
